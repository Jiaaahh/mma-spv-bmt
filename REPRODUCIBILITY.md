# Reproducibility scope

## Supported in the public distribution

- SHA-256 integrity of public distribution files and retained E01–E32 maps.
- Saved-prediction label recount, main-result export and selected statistical replays, as actually validated in `validation/step4_candidate_validation.json`.
- Historical configuration, source and source-to-public distribution provenance inspection.

## Archived Step 3 results

With the complete controlled inputs, Step 3 replayed C1/E1/StudyB MMA inference for 91,800 predictions, primary statistics, selected comparator/Point analyses, 40 historical cost arrays and 10 figure PNGs. The report in `archived_audit/` is explicitly historical.

## Omitted inputs and remaining limits

No raw fonts, frozen glyph bitmaps, per-glyph stimulation/selection arrays, visual figure examples, reconstructed prototypes, pretrained weights, Point checkpoints or G09 checkpoints are included. Therefore this public distribution does not support exact end-to-end model inference or pixel-identical figure regeneration. It supports numerical verification from saved predictions. A future rights-cleared asset supplement would need its own manifest, author approval and replay validation. Rendering different open fonts is a new run and must not be labelled the original experiment.

The six Point weights are unavailable and the four selected G09 weights unverified; missing Point vendor sources are listed separately. Other model inference and neural training were not comprehensively replayed. Historical latency arrays are not a fresh hardware measurement. E01–E32 preservation does not mean every model or all 36 supplementary tables have been independently rerun.

Whole-glyph and common-window protocols remain distinct. Point results average scalar accuracy over three initializations, not logits or votes. Font/mapping panels and familiar character classes bound generalization. Primary intervals use 180 paired character clusters conditional on the stated cells. Negative gains, Point outperforming MMA in the common window, and exploratory/post-hoc identities remain in the numerical evidence and experiment maps. The alternative multi-axis C1 interval is not substituted for the historical interval.
